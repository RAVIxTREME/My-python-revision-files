Q2(e) / PYQ: Explain the disastrous effects of plastic carry bags on the environment. (4M)
1. Non-biodegradable nature: Plastic stays in soil for centuries, destroying soil fertility and choking underground water channels.
2. Harm to marine life: Discarded plastic reaches oceans, where aquatic animals and birds mistake it for food, leading to starvation and death.
3. Toxic emissions: Burning plastic releases dangerous carcinogenic gases that cause severe air pollution and respiratory diseases.
4. Clogged drainage: Plastic waste clogs urban drainage systems, causing artificial floods during monsoons.

Q4(a) / PYQ: Write a technical description of the 'Smart Watch' (75 words)
A smartwatch is a wearable computerized wristwatch with advanced computing and communication capabilities. It features a digital OLED touchscreen display, optical heart rate sensors, an accelerometer, microphone, speaker, and a rechargeable lithium-ion battery. Powered by an embedded microchip and operating system, it connects via Bluetooth to smartphones. Smartwatches track daily fitness metrics like step count, sleep quality, and blood oxygen levels, while allowing users to receive call notifications, messages, and manage schedules on the go.
Q4(b) / PYQ: Write a technical description of the 'Electric Bike (E-Bike)' (75 words)
An electric bike is a motorized bicycle equipped with an integrated electric motor and rechargeable battery to assist propulsion. Its main components include a brushless DC motor, lithium-ion battery pack, throttle, pedal-assist sensor, controller unit, and digital dashboard. Power from the battery drives the motor, reducing physical pedaling effort. E-bikes offer eco-friendly urban commuting, adjustable speed modes, regenerative braking, and zero tailpipe emissions, making them an efficient sustainable transportation alternative.
Q4(c) / PYQ: Write a technical description of the 'Bluetooth Speaker' (75 words)
A Bluetooth speaker is a portable wireless audio playback device designed for amplifying sound from digital media. It consists of internal audio drivers, a digital-to-analog converter, an amplifier circuit, a Bluetooth receiver module, control buttons, and a rechargeable battery. Encased in a durable plastic or metal chassis, it pairs wirelessly with smartphones and laptops. It allows users to stream music and audiobooks outdoors without cables, offering high fidelity sound in a compact form factor.

1. Gestures & Indications: Pointing finger indicates direction or emphasis; open palms indicate honesty and transparency; clenched fists indicate anger or determination; nodding indicates agreement.
2. Importance of Facial Expressions: A warm smile builds rapport, eye contact conveys confidence and sincerity, and expressive features prevent monotony during presentations.
3. Tips for Effective PPT: Follow the 6×6 rule (max 6 bullets per slide, 6 words per line); use high color contrast; maintain font size above 24pt; avoid clutter and distracting animations.
